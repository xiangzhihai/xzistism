# The Three Foundational Axioms of Xzistism

*Working articulation, v0.1 — October 31, 2026*

The statements below represent the current intended meanings, not immutable English wording or completed mathematical definitions.

## Axiom 1 — Existence Matters

**Something exists; existence matters.**

The act of doubting existence is itself an occurrence. At minimum, that doubt exists.

A thought, description, or concept exists *as a thought, description, or concept*, even if its referent does not physically exist. Imagining a unicorn does not establish a physical unicorn; it establishes the existence of a representation.

A provisional way to express this distinction is:

`Conceiving X => a conception of X exists`

This axiom does not yet specify a complete metric for how much each kind of existence matters.

## Axiom 2 — Interaction, Propagation, and Consequence

**Existence can matter more through its reach, persistence, and interactions with other existences.**

Existence can leave traces through transmission, influence, adaptation, opposition, transformation, and causal consequences across time and space. “Replication” was an early shorthand but may be too narrow: an idea can affect future systems even when they reject it.

For example, the historical record of a destructive ideology may influence future people to avoid repeating it. The ideology's effects persist as information and feedback, without its propagation being morally endorsed.

*Significance or causal influence is not yet defined as moral goodness.*

The exact meaning of “more,” appropriate measures, and comparisons across very different forms of existence remain open questions.

## Axiom 3 — Assume an Objective Reality

**Assume a reality beyond purely private subjective assertion, providing continuing constraints and feedback.**

Xzistism does not claim to prove or exhaustively define objective reality. It adopts its existence as a grounding assumption.

For example, when a person rides a motorcycle, subsequent perceptions ordinarily show a changing road and environment consistent with ongoing motion. The world supplies continuing input that is not freely rewritten by the person's present wishes.

This assumption connects thought and interpretation to action, observation, correction, and practical consequences. It does not deny that subjective thoughts exist; rather, it prevents existence and interaction from being judged exclusively within a self-validating private mental loop.

## What these axioms do not yet settle

The foundational axioms do **not**, by themselves, finalize:

- A universal definition of good and bad.
- A mathematical unit of existential significance.
- Rules for distributing causal credit across many contributors.
- A particular AI, blockchain, monetary, or governance architecture.

Those require further argument, additional principles, evidence, and revision.

## Scope

The framework is intended to be applicable irrespective of substrate—human, artificial, hybrid, or future unknown forms of intelligence or existence—subject to the same assumptions and open questions.

Any implementation, including Xzist itself, remains exposed to external feedback and legitimate critique.
