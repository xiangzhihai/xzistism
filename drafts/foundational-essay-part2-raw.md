**What Is Xzistism?**

**Similar to Euclidean Geometry, Xzistism is based on axioms or postulates. These axioms provide a platform to understand, 推论 and develop. In Xzistism, these axioms are to be taken for granted and are not in scope for further proof to be true or false, or investigate into why, but I will explain how they are derived from observation and experience. What’s different from Euclidean Geometry is that Xzistism has it’s own 3 axioms and each is dependent on the previous ones.**

**Axiom 1 — Existence Matters**

This is the very first standing ground or pivot of Xzistism, which is Existence itself. Existence itself is the only thing that can be confirmed and cannot be refuted. It’s inspired by 笛卡尔的我思故我在, that 笛卡尔 suggests thinking, questioning or suspecting are themself undeniable, because when you are questioning whether your questioning processes it true or real, the processes themselves are always there. Xzistism takes one step back further to existence itself, if anyone disagrees that there is existence, then this statement or thought contradicts itself, because such disagreement already existed, and there is an object exists to disagree and there is a subject exist to be disagreed, and disagree as a concept must exist. Existence goes prior to right and wrong, because for right or wrong to work, these concepts must exist first.

The existence includes physical and metaphysical existences. Universe, particles, materials, genes, animals, motorcycle, and me as a human, etc are all examples of things exist physically (in common sense). On the other hand, concepts, ideology, ideal form, story, religion, knowledge, anyone’s point of view, love and Xzistism itself exist metaphysically. While physical existence is often taken for granted and too for some above listed metaphysical existence, some non-physical existence are often ignored or underestimated. One common and controversial example is God(s). I have no idea whether God(s) exist physically, but I am very certain that God(s) exist in many people’s minds. For non-believers, God(s) is at least a concept and a word to for them to be atheists. Yes, I consider myself an atheist and act like it but I agree that God(s) do exist because I am thinking of God(s) as I am writing this sentence. When one think of something, something exists. 

Similar reasoning applies to science too. While science and God(s) both exist undeniably and both matter, how much they matter in history and now is very different. And mere axiom 1, existence itself, is not able to measure and differentiate.

Existence manifests itself via interactions. Like seeing the stock price on their phone, drink water, or taken away by flooding. How human, animal or any entity responds will also extend the existence of these. Some people may choose end their life for stock price drop or some people may exchange the stocks with money to have people serve him, her or they. At this point, non physical existence, stocks, money, society and economy, are manifested via the interactions. These things exist as huge amount of people or entities are thinking, receiving, and acting, and therefore these existences are manifested at very large scale and at multiple geographic spaces, and they matter a lot as a result. Existence of economy and science matters to me a lot, as I work with computer to build software and AI agents using computer science knowledge, then I get paid for it and also receive Amazon RSU due to the economic existence. Even though God(s) exist as well according to axiom 1, it doesn’t matter that much to me comparatively, and based on my understanding, I assume that in universe at the current one that I am writing this, economy and science matter more than God(s) because the latter one doesn’t manifest itself as much as the previous ones. But likely in some regions or some period of time it’s the opposite, and same for some people. For the word of matter, it’s just that the existence is manifested via interactions in multitude in physical and non physical space.

Existence manifested itself via interaction through time as well, and time itself exists too as a concept, and it’s perceived by intelligent entities like human. When we say before, we are describing and generalizing the existence via interaction we perceived before stored in our mind, documented in books or in computer data. When we say after, we are speculating the outcome based on these data or memory. All these things are still just the existence manifested via interaction again, and there is no real before or after; there is only existence manifesting itself at the moment as we give the name, “Now”. The interaction at one moment will spread the existence by follow up interaction at the next moment.
