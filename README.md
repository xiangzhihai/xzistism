# Xzistism

**An evolving philosophical framework grounded in existence, interaction, and an assumed objective reality.**

Xzistism is a philosophical starting point, not a finished moral theory or a prescribed implementation. Its first three axioms provide a shared foundation; future principles, interpretations, and applications can be developed by different people, organizations, or intelligent entities.

## Three foundational axioms

1. **Existence matters.** Even a thought or concept exists in some form; conceptual existence must not be confused with physical existence.
2. **Interaction and propagation can extend what matters.** An existence can leave effects across time and space through influence, transmission, transformation, and interaction. This does not automatically mean that greater influence is morally better.
3. **Assume an objective reality.** The framework assumes a reality not arbitrarily determined by an individual's present subjective beliefs, providing continuing constraints and feedback. This is an assumption, not a claim to have conclusively proved external reality.

See [AXIOMS.md](AXIOMS.md) for explanation and open questions.

## Xzistism and Xzist

**Xzistism** is the general philosophical foundation.

**Xzist** is one proposed practical implementation, initially exploring causal contribution, persistent attribution, and adaptive mathematical objectives. It does not define or monopolize Xzistism. Other implementations may independently develop from the same axioms, disagree with Xzist, or eventually surpass it.
