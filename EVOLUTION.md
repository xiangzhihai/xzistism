## 2026 — Three foundational axioms articulated

1. Existence matters.
2. Interaction and propagation can increase an existence's significance, including indirect effects through rejection, learning, or transformation.
3. Assume an objective reality that supplies ongoing feedback beyond private subjective assertions.

The first three axioms are currently intended to remain conceptually foundational, even while phrasing and analysis improve.

## 2026 — Xzist as one implementation direction

A separate, evolving project idea, **Xzist**, proposes to explore a mathematical objective/loss function and a system of causal contribution attribution.

Initial technology directions discussed include AI-assisted causal estimation and durable/verifiable records; these are speculative options rather than commitments. The intended relationship to money is initially complementary: link to, extend, and improve existing systems, with substitution considered only if demonstrated useful.

## Open questions for future versions

- How can existential significance be compared without mistaking influence for moral goodness?
- How should credit flow backward through complex, branching causal histories?
- How can a system learn from downstream outcomes while resisting reward hacking?
- What kinds of evidence can ground attribution and revision in objective-world feedback?
- How can the framework preserve legitimate historical credit without guaranteeing privileged rewards to its founder?

Future edits should describe what changed, why, and what evidence or arguments prompted the revision.
