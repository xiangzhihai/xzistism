## Retrospective perspective — October 31, 2026

Zhihai describes the early record as an initial form of his thinking about the possibility that future AI might understand and begin acting according to ideas later developed as Xzistism and Xzist.

He now regards the three foundational axioms as providing a framework in which such interaction could be interpreted.

**Important historical distinction:** the 2026 formulation should not be presented as the exact language of 2024 without independently examining the original image. A historical antecedent and a later explicit definition are not the same document.

## From thought to public framework

The current three-axiom formulation emerged through extended reflection and dialogue involving existence, recursion, feedback, propagation, objective grounding, and causal attribution.

The purpose of this repository is to make that development explicit, inspectable, and citable.

An origin record establishes evidence of a documented contribution. It does not by itself establish universal priority over all similar ideas, nor prescribe how all future downstream credit must be allocated.

## Attribution and continued development

Xzistism aims to make room for independent versions, improvements, critiques, and successors. The contribution of an earlier source can remain part of a later system's causal history even if the later system changes, disputes, or surpasses that source.

A future attribution implementation may attempt to estimate such contributions. That estimation must remain distinct from the historical fact of authorship and open to evidence and revision.
